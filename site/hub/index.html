● Cortex Hub · voice assistant / LLM · Windows

Your day, one word away.

Say “Cortex” and ask. Speech is recognised on your PC, answered by Claude with your own account and spoken back. The dashboard keeps your music, timers, reminders, shopping list, mail and weather in one place.

Install

One installer, no administrator rights needed. Windows may warn you because it is not signed.

  1. Run CortexHubSetup.exe

    Choose the language and the folder.

  2. Pick your widgets

    Tick the cards you want on the panel. You can change them later.

  3. Pick your accounts

    Claude, Spotify, email or none. The installer never asks for a password.

  4. Sign in, one at a time

    Cortex opens a wizard that guides each login. You can skip any of them.

You need a microphone and speakers for voice. The speech model downloads the first time.

Accounts you can connect

All of them are optional.

Claude

The assistant runs on Claude Code with your own account. The wizard opens its official sign-in.

Spotify

Uses a free Spotify developer app that you create, plus Spotify's own sign-in page. Playback control needs Premium.

Gmail

Uses a Google app password that you can revoke at any time.

WhatsApp

Experimental and not in every build. You choose read only, or read and write. It links by QR code through an unofficial client, so WhatsApp could restrict the account, more so if you send messages.

What stays on your PC

  • Voice The microphone stays on while the assistant waits for “Cortex”, and it can wake by mistake. You can pause it at any time. Speech is recognised locally; the text goes to Claude with your account, and replies are read aloud through Microsoft Edge's online voices.
  • Keys and passwords Kept on your PC. This site has no server and no accounts.
  • Uninstalling Removes the program and keeps your data, and tells you which folder to delete if you want it gone.
