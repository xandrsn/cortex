Your hands are the shortcut.

Cortex Sense watches your webcam, finds your hands and turns a gesture into a command on your PC. A swipe skips the song, two open palms minimise everything, a rock sign runs your own script. You choose what each movement does.

Try it with your cameraDownload for Windows

v0.3.0 · Windows 10/11, 64-bit · Free and open source (MIT)

How it works

Three steps, all on your computer.

  1. See the hand

    MediaPipe finds 21 points on each hand in every frame of the webcam.

  2. Read the gesture

    29 built-in gestures: poses (thumb up, fist, OK, rock…), motions (swipes, flicks, circles, a wave) and two-handed ones. Or record your own.

  3. Run the action

    The gesture runs the command you picked for it, after a short hold so it does not fire by accident.

A calibration wizard measures your camera, light, hand size and pinch first, so it works at your desk. Gestures can be limited to when you are looking at the screen.

Any movement, any action

Pick from a list, or go as far as your own code.

Windows

Minimise everything, show the desktop, maximise, close, switch window, lock the PC. Pinch to grab a window and drop it on half the screen.

Music and volume

Play, pause, next, previous, volume, mute. Works with Spotify, the browser or any player.

Apps and websites

Open a program, a file or a URL.

Keyboard shortcuts

Any combination, like ctrl+shift+m to mute a call or win+shift+s for a screenshot.

Your own scripts

Run a PowerShell, Python or batch file with arguments: start a focus mode, deploy a build, switch your monitors.

Your own gestures

Hold a pose for three seconds and Sense learns it from your hand (nearest neighbours on normalised hand points).

Install

Download CortexSenseSetup.exe from the releases page. No administrator rights needed; Windows may warn you because it is not signed. It shows a webcam privacy notice, can start with Windows and opens the calibration.

Needs a webcam. Also compatible with Cortex Hub, a voice assistant, if you use it; Sense works on its own.

What stays on your PC

  • Video: every frame is processed on your computer and never stored or sent. The web demo works the same way, inside your browser tab.
  • Settings: your gestures and actions are saved in a file next to the program. Only the local panel can change them.
