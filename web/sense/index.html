Pick your camera

Sense processes video on this computer only; nothing is uploaded. Choose the webcam you face.

Look at the screen

Sit the way you usually sit and look at the middle of the screen for 3 seconds. Sense learns what "looking at the screen" means for your camera position and checks light and frame rate.

Show your open hand

Raise one open hand at your normal distance and keep it still for 3 seconds. Sense measures how big your hand looks, so swipes need the same effort near or far from the camera.

Pinch a few times

Touch thumb and index together and open them again, several times, for 4 seconds. This sets when a pinch grabs a window and when it lets go.

    Gestures → commands

    Hub actions run in Cortex Hub when it is open; without Hub, music actions fall back to the media keys. Shortcuts look like ctrl+shift+m; programs can be a path or a URL.

    Your own gestures

    Hold a pose for 3 seconds; Sense learns it from your own hand (k-nearest neighbours on normalised landmarks) and lists it above so you can give it a command.
